News

The project record: what was measured on air, what was decided about the architecture and what changed in the product. Every entry carries a date and stands on a concrete result, not on an announcement.

September 2026

01.09.2026 Company

Investment raised to integrate the underslung pod on a carrier

The round closes exactly the task we have been calling the main one: put GR-1 on a 15-inch copter acting as a relay carrier, wire up power and comms, and take the pairing through to repeatable flights.

August 2026

31.08.2026 On air

Controller hunting exercised on the live station

The scanner takes a bearing on the operator, not on the airframe. Every bearing in the log was measured by MUSIC on the five-channel array — none was computed from coordinates.

31.08.2026 Front end

The board holds a gapless stream to 9.5 MS/s over a dedicated LAN

The ceiling is about 9.7. The network turned out not to be the bottleneck it was assumed to be.

30.08.2026 Link

The s.Link symbol rate is settled: 25 000 sym/s

It also closed a documentation discrepancy that turned out never to have existed.

29.08.2026 Architecture

The operator console became the single operator surface

The s.Link channel carries 962 bit/s — a mapper page does not fit through it by three orders of magnitude, and the station's Wi-Fi is management-only in the field.

28.08.2026 Front end

The 8 kHz line in the spectrum turned out to be the station's own USB

Not an external source and not interference — the stream of our own KrakenSDR.

27.08.2026 Limits

Two sources in one channel produce a confident bearing onto empty space

The array does not split a 62° separation: instead of two directions you get one, and it looks trustworthy. The limit is documented so it is not mistaken for a target.

26.08.2026 Direction finding

One second of dwell gives the same bearing as thirty

The old 30-second floor was about record quality, not about direction.

26.08.2026 Scanners

A chirped-radar recognition path in the 2700–3100 MHz band

The presets were checked against real air, not only against synthetic signals.

24.08.2026 Architecture

The LoRa mesh is retired: reporting runs over our own s.Link, point to point

An owner decision. Multi-station cross-bearing stays — but it is the operator console that does it, not a node-to-node network.

22.08.2026 Code provenance

pyargus removed — the direction estimators are our own

Bartlett, Capon, MEM and FBA are written in house. One GPL dependency fewer on the direct import path.

22.08.2026 Code provenance

iq_header and shmemIface rewritten clean

Two files left the direct import path. Where a specification compresses the code, a clean room works; where it does not, the check said so itself.

22.08.2026 Research

A route worked out for taking 6 GHz boards up to 12 GHz

Undersampling does not help — the RF front end itself is the limit. It needs a mixer with a preselector, which means separate hardware.

And immediately failed the fifth gate — which was the point: a criterion is there to catch things.
