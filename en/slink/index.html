s.Link — telemetry, commands and video

ELINTIR radio solutions connecting a station to its operator. Telemetry, commands and video within a shared control environment.

Overview · Applications · Validation · Modulations · Adaptation · Integration

Overview

Communications for the platform’s task

s.Link · telemetry and commands
A radio channel for equipment status, observation data and commands between a station and its operator.
s.Link4k · video
A separate radio-link development direction for video delivery. The transport combines data protection and loss recovery, with video presented in a shared operator console.
Data protection
The stack implements encryption and data authentication. Integration parameters and configuration are agreed for each application.

Applications

From telemetry to video links

Fixed and mobile systems
Data exchange between equipment and operator, status display and remote interaction.
Ground robots
We study video-link profiles for built environments and multipath propagation.
Airborne platforms
We assess video-link profiles for line-of-sight channels and changing propagation conditions.

Validation

From models to trials

Radio path
The telemetry channel and video-file delivery have been tested through a real radio path.
Software stack
Modulations, transport and adaptation mechanisms are evaluated with software tests and channel modelling.
Application assessment
The solution scope and readiness of individual features are clarified during technical discussions. Model results and over-the-air trials are treated separately.

Modulations

Different conditions, different waveforms

GMSK
Used in the s.Link telemetry direction for short messages and commands.
BPSK, QPSK and QAM
Options evaluated in software models for different balances of throughput and channel requirements. The baseline video profile has also been tested through a radio path.
CSS and phase hybrids
Chirp signals and selected hybrid variants have been studied in software models for short-message delivery.
COFDM for ground platforms
Assessed in channel models with reflections and multipath propagation. The results support further investigation for ground-robot video links.

Adaptation

Matching video to channel capacity

Delivery assessment
The software stack implements available-rate estimation using data-reception results.
Video-quality selection
A software controller selects quality and bitrate according to assessed channel capacity. Behaviour under changing conditions has test coverage.
Coordinated changes
Profile-control mechanisms account for interaction between sender and receiver. They are evaluated as part of the shared transport and video stack.
In-house implementation
Control over the physical layer, transport and software lets us develop them together for a customer’s platform. This supports flexible integration; we do not claim comparative range or latency advantages without matched trials.
